### Chapter 18. Your Systems Strategy
- A system is a set of things, actions, ideas, and information that interact with each other, and in so doing, alter other systems.
	- In short, everything is a system.
- **Three Kinds of Systems in Business** : Hard Systems, Soft Systems, and Information Systems
	- Hard Systems are inanimate, unliving things 
		- e.g. computer, Lucite collar around the white board
	- Soft Systems are either animate(living) or ideas (e.g. You, script)
	- Information Systems are those that provide us with information about the interaction between the other two (e.g. inventory control, sales activity summary reports)
- Business Development Program is about Innovation, Quantification, Orchestration, and integration of these three kinds of systems in your business
- Will applied to any conflict creates energy. Conflict without will creates frustration. Conflict with will creates resolution.
- A selling system is a **Soft Systems** (Power Point Selling System)
	1. Identification of the specific Benchmarks or consumer decision points in your selling process
	2. The literal scripting of the words that will get you to each one successfully 
	3. The creation of the various materials to be used with each script.
	4. The memorization of each Benchmark’s script.
	5. The delivery of each script by your salespeople in identical fashion.
	6. Leaving your people to communicate more effectively, by articulating,  watching, listening, hearing, acknowledging, understanding, and engaging each and every prospect as fully as he needs to be.
- The Power Point Selling System is composed of two parts: 
	1) Structure is what you do.
	- predetermined elements of the Process, and includes exactly what you say, the materials you use when you say it, and what you wear.
	2) Substance is how you do it.
	- what you—the salesperson—bring to the Process, and includes how you say it, how you use it when you say it, and how you are when you say it.

#### THE POWER POINT SELLING PROCESS
- The Power Point Selling Process is a series of scripts defining the entire interaction between the salesperson and the customer
	1. The Appointment Presentation
		- The purpose of an Appointment Presentation is to make an appointment to move the prospect from where he is to the second Benchmark in the process, the Needs Analysis Presentation
	2. The Needs Analysis Presentation
		1) Restablish the emotional commitment
		2) tell the prospect how you would like to proceed to fulfill your promise to him
		3) establish your credibility in the prospect’s mind by communicating your company’s expertise and your personal willingness to do whatever is necessary to utilize that expertise on his behalf
		4) describe the product and why it works so well
		5) complete the Questionnaire
		6) provide the prospective customer with the information he promised
		7) make an appointment with the prospective customer to return with the product
	3. The Solutions Presentation
		- Provide the rational armament for the emotional commitment
		- Selling isn't “closing.” Selling is opening
	- Doing this process the same way every single time, you will not have a
selling person but a selling system(soft system).
- The information should be recorded manually or as a database on your computer.
	- The Information System could tell you the things you need to
know! Things you don’t know now.
		- If your Systems Strategy is the glue that holds your Franchise Prototype together, then information is the glue that holds your Systems
Strategy together. It tells you when and why you need to change
- Your Primary Aim and your Strategic Objective and your Organizational Strategy and your Management Strategy and your People Strategy and your Marketing Strategy and your Systems Strategy—all of them are totally interdependent, rather than independent of one another
