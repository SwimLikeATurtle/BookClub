### Chapter 14. Your Organizational Strategy
- "All organizations are hierarchical. At each level people serve under those above them. An organization is therefore a structured institution. If it is not structured, it is a mob. Mobs do not get things done, they destroy things." - Theodore Levitt, Management for Business Growth
- The organizational development reflected in the Organization Chart can have a more profound impact on a small company than any other single Business Development step.

	- **Organizing Around Personalities**
	    - Unfortunately, personalities, good feelings, goodwill, and luck aren’t the only ingredients of a successful organization; alone, they are the recipe for chaos and disaster.

	- **Organizing Your Company**
	1) The first thing to do is to think about the business as a corporation, rather than as a partnership.
	    - Rather than thinking of themselves as partners, they now think of themselves as shareholders
	2) The founders each visualizes how he/she would like his/her life to look and write the 'Primary Aim' under their name
	3) The founders agree with each other that their role outside of the business is to be the shareholders and inside of the business is to be employees
	4) Create the Strategic Objective for the company
	    - In doing so, collect info about the consumer, the competition, and the pricing
	5) Create the Organization Chart
	    - Describe all the work that’s going to be done in the company when its full potential is realized w/o taking into consideration how many employees you have at the moment
	6) Write a Position Contract
	    - Position Contract: a contract with a summary of the results to be achieved by each position in the company
	7) Assign the roles 
	    - Assign members of the company to each role and have them sign the Position Contract

	- **Prototyping the Position: Replacing Yourself with a System**
	1) Start building their business by looking at each position in the business as though it were a Franchise Prototype of its own.
		- Start at the bottom(positions) of the business and work your way up top, not as the owners of the company but as the employees doing Tactical Work.
			- Tactical Work is the work all technicians do. Strategic Work is the work their managers do.
	2) Think about the customers and the employees 
        - Think “What would best serve our customer here? How could I most easily give the customer what he wants while also maximizing profits for the company? And at the same time, how could I give the person responsible for that work the best possible experience?”
	3) Create an Operations Manual and start hiring
	    - After making an Operations Manual for a role, hire a person who is eager to learn how to do it right rather than someone with experience to do the job.
	    - This will free the founders of the Tactical Work of his business and help him focus on managing the system.
	    - After hiring for one position move on to another position and repeat

- Your Organization Chart flows down from your Strategic Objective, which in turn flows down from your Primary Aim.
- “And that’s what this whole process of organizational development is.
It’s the process through which you think through your business as best as you’re able and then structure the way it is to work. Your Organization Chart is that structure. It is you talking to your people and the world, telling them exactly how you see your business working when it’s done. When the dream is in place. It’s you sharing your mind with the world. And then, once having shared it, it’s you telling your people and the world that you believe enough in the vision to live it yourself!

### Chapter 15. Your Management Strategy 
- The success of a company's management strategy does not depend on finding competent managers
	- Instead, the Management System will become the means through which your Franchise Prototype produces the results you want

- **What Is a Management System?**
- It is a System designed into your Prototype to produce a marketing
result. And the more automatic that System is, the more effective your
Franchise Prototype will be.
	- It isn’t a management tool, it’s a marketing tool. Thus, its purpose is not just to create an efficient Prototype but an effective
one which finds and keeps customers better than any other

- The case of the resort hotel located in the redwood grove is shared to show how management system is implemented IRL:
	- Making people feel heard is the most important thing for business and for relationships.
	- "You’d be amazed at how many people come up to me after staying here just to thank me for how well they were treated. But it’s not the big things they talk about; it’s always the little things." - The manager at the Hotel
